# Campus Bus Tracker

Prompt 1 — Direct Structure Match



"Build a landing page for 'PUB Bus Track' (Pundra University Bus Tracking System), closely following the layout structure of transitapp.com.



Navbar: PUB Bus Track bus-icon logo on the left, nav links in the center — Home, Live Location, Buses, Time Schedule, Routes — and Login / Sign Up buttons on the right, styled as rounded pill buttons.



Background: light sky-blue gradient background with soft white clouds, matching a bright, airy feel.



Hero section (centered): Bold headline 'Your Ride to Campus, On Time' with 'Campus' or a key word in an orange/yellow accent color. Subtext below: 'Track your university bus in real-time — buses, routes, and schedules in one place.' A green rounded CTA button: 'Get Started'.



Left side illustration: a university building/hostel illustration with a bus stop sign, a student character with a backpack, and small sticker-style icons (books, ID card, bus ticket).



Right side illustration: a phone mockup showing a live map with a moving bus icon, an ETA card floating like '12 minutes — Arrive at 9:15', and a hand tapping a 'GO' button, plus a bus illustration at the bottom with the route name on its side.



Add a 'Download PUB Bus Track App' button/section near the hero with app store style badges.



Add smooth entrance animations: illustrations fade/slide in on load, the ETA card and map pin have a subtle floating animation, and the bus icon animates moving along a dotted route line continuously.



Fully responsive, playful illustrated style, same visual energy as the reference site."
